About Haddy's Creations

[PHOTO: Hayden (and maybe Haddy) with the printer]

Hi! I'm Hayden. Haddy's Creations is named after my infant daughter — she's the reason I started making things, and the reason I want to build something of my own.

I got into 3D printing with a Bambu Lab P2S and quickly learned there's a lot that beginners aren't told. So I write the guides I wish I'd had: honest, simple and based on what I've actually printed.

I also print small, practical items to order in the shop.
